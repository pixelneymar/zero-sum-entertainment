# Betting overlay: research and design

Why this layout: the owner wants the footage full-bleed with the betting UI as an overlay that never fights the action. Five research agents surveyed live-prediction overlays (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok LIVE), in-play sportsbooks, broadcast side-pick graphics, mobile ergonomics over video and overlay accessibility; three layouts were proposed and scored by three judges. The winner, a notched quick-bet sheet, is implemented in `symbols/components/BetSheet.js` and `symbols/pages/main.js`.

## Principles applied

- Anchor the vote widget to an edge band, never over the centre or the bottom-centre of the frame. Every chat-rail platform pins it to the TOP of the rail; TikTok, the only one that draws on the video, uses a thin band at the top of the split screen and a contributor strip at the bottom. For Zero Sum the free zones are the top band (challengers' heads are at mid-height, scales at y 78-100%) and the far left/right columns. (https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/channel-points-predictions ; https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/how-to-use-polls ; https://help.kick.com/en/articles/11043577-guide-to-predictions-for-viewers ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2524549 ; https://accountssale.com/how-do-tiktok-live-pk-battles-work/ ; https://www.tiktok.com/live/studio/help/article/Monetize-your-creativity/Start-a-LIVE-match-to-call-on-your-supporters?lang=en)
- Use three footprints: a one-line banner (CTA + countdown) by default, an expanded panel only on demand, and a one-line receipt after the vote. The panel must collapse by itself after the commit; the receipt shows the chosen side and the time left. (https://stream-rise.com/blog/channel-points-predictions ; https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/how-to-use-polls ; https://help.kick.com/en/articles/11043577-guide-to-predictions-for-viewers)
- Make the commit a two-step select-then-confirm, and state that the vote is final before the confirm tap. All five products forbid a change of side after submission. (https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/how-to-use-polls ; https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15268877 ; https://help.kick.com/en/articles/11043577-guide-to-predictions-for-viewers ; https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/channel-points-predictions)
- Model LOCKED as its own named state between OPEN and RESOLVED, with its own timestamp and its own visual. In this state nothing on the widget may move: no new votes, no pool change, no counter animation. This is the exact contract Zero Sum promises for its 5 s freeze. (https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/predictions/ ; https://help.kick.com/en/articles/11043577-guide-to-predictions-for-viewers ; https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/how-to-use-polls)
- Keep the bet slip inside the video player. Do not move it to a strip below or to a separate screen. For ZSE: the dock is a glass overlay on the 16:9 stage; the viewer never looks away from the footage. (https://www.geniussports.com/content-hub/betvision-in-play-betting-engagement/ (read) and https://www.geniussports.com/bet/bet-vision/ (read))
- Put the tap target where the subject is in the frame. For ZSE: the Challenger 1 card sits over the left third and the Challenger 2 card over the right third, at the challengers' chest height, so a tap on a person is a tap on that side. Leave the centre column and the scale zone (x 38-62%, y 78-100%) free of glass. (https://www.geniussports.com/content-hub/betvision-in-play-betting-engagement/ (read))
- Selection to confirmation must take one or two taps. With a fixed 20-chip stake there is no stake step, so tap 1 = select a challenger, tap 2 = Place bet. Offer a 'Quick bet' setting that makes tap 1 place the bet; keep the two-tap flow as the default because there is no cancel after lock. (https://altenar.com/blog/how-to-design-a-sportsbook-user-experience-ux-that-wins-in-live-play/ (read); https://helpcentre.sportsbet.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/18424099184141-What-Is-Quick-Bet (search snippet; page returned 403); https://betting.betfair.com/betfair-announcements/fill-or-kill-091213-6.html and https://support.betfair.com/app/answers/detail/10310-exchange-how-to-place-a-bet/ (search snippets; pages returned 403))
- Show the stake and the return on the commit control before the tap. For ZSE the button reads 'Place 20 chips on Challenger 2' and the card shows 'Return ≈ 37 chips' computed from the live pot after rake. The rake note becomes one number, not a sentence. (https://www.bethq.com/articles/how-to-bet-on-bet365/ (read); https://startpolymarket.com/guides/how-to-trade/ (read); https://prometteursolutions.com/blog/user-experience-and-interface-in-sports-betting-apps/ (read))
- Treat the two challengers and the scales as the action-safe zone, and put every overlay element in the outer ring of the frame. Broadcast safe-area standards exist so graphics never sit on essential action: SMPTE ST 2046-1 sets safe action at 93% and safe title at 90% of the frame; EBU R95 sets 3.5% action-safe and 5% graphics-safe per edge. For Zero Sum, invert the usual reading: the protected region is the challengers (left-centre and right-centre) plus the scales (x 38-62%, y 78-100%). Overlay geometry: a top strip no taller than 12% of video height, left and right rails no wider than 18% each and stopping above y 76%, and the bottom-centre band x 30-70%, y 70-100% kept empty in every phase. (https://helpcenter.xr.global/hc/en-us/articles/23817307323028-Safe-Title-Area ; https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r095)
- Anchor persistent bottom UI to the bottom-left and bottom-right corners at the title-safe inset, never bottom-centre. Broadcast lower-thirds are left-aligned and start at the 10% inset (192 px from the left and 108 px from the bottom at 1920x1080) because the bottom-centre lane is reserved for captions, which North American rules require to be centre-justified inside the 90% safe area, and for the station bug. In Zero Sum the bottom-centre lane is the scales. Put the Challenger 1 card at bottom-left and the Challenger 2 card at bottom-right, each ending before x 30% and after x 70%, with the Place bet button inside the selected card rather than as a centred primary button. (https://infinitecreation.io/tutorial-lower-thirds ; https://helpcenter.xr.global/hc/en-us/articles/5760339463444-North-American-Closed-Captioning-Requirements ; https://closedcaptionservice.com/blog-title-safe-lower-thirds.html)
- Mirror the overlay to the physical position of the contestants: side 1 on the left edge, side 2 on the right edge, the neutral countdown in the middle. This is the fighting-game HUD convention that viewers already know, and the same left/right/top structure that Valorant and CS broadcasts use (score and timer top-centre, per-team state on the sides). The intuitive cue is spatial, not textual: a viewer must be able to tell which card belongs to which person without reading a name. (https://samm1996blog.wordpress.com/2016/01/06/hud-designs-in-games/ ; https://medium.com/@janushshah/the-5-year-journey-of-valorants-broadcast-ui-from-clutter-to-clarity-f0bc4b60c46d)
- Put the one decision-critical number top-centre, and never remove it. The LCS HUD moved gold to the centre of the top bar because the top bar "should contain the most critical information" and new viewers were reading the wrong number (kills). In Zero Sum the decision-critical number is the seconds until lock plus the phase word; the pot is second. Keep the countdown dial top-centre in every phase and change only its glyph (clock during betting, padlock during locked, checkmark during results). Riot removed gold difference in the 2025 HUD and had to reinstate it after feedback; do not hide the countdown during reveal. (https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/summer-broadcast-updates-the-hud ; https://www.espn.com/gaming/story/_/id/29297696/how-made-new-lcs-hud ; https://esports.gg/news/league-of-legends/new-lol-esports-hud-debut-first-stand-2025/)
- Put the two challenger targets in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners of the frame, not along the bottom edge. Recommended: Challenger 1 card at x 0-36%, Challenger 2 card at x 64-100%, both at y 70-100%. This leaves the scales (x 38-62%, y 78-100%) fully visible and maps each card to the side its challenger stands on. (https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2013/02/how-do-users-really-hold-mobile-devices.php ; https://alistapart.com/article/how-we-hold-our-gadgets/ ; https://inairspace.com/blogs/learn-with-inair/godot-touch-controls-for-mobile-a-complete-practical-guide-1 ; https://patents.google.com/patent/US20150116593A1/en)
- Treat the centre-bottom 24% of width and the two mid-height bands where the challengers stand as a no-UI safe zone, the same way TikTok and Reels reserve a centre band for the subject. Recommended: define a CSS 'subject mask' (x 38-62% by y 78-100% for the scales; x 15-40% and 60-85% by y 15-75% for the bodies) and assert in a layout test that no overlay box intersects it. (https://zeely.ai/blog/tiktok-safe-zones/ ; https://www.firstpier.com/resources/instagram-ad-safe-zones ; https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video)
- On portrait phones keep the 16:9 video in fit-to-screen (letterboxed) mode and put the bet dock in the letterbox band below the video as a standard (non-modal) bottom sheet. Do not crop the video to fill the portrait screen. Recommended: video at 100% width (about 221 px tall on a 393 px wide phone), sheet anchored directly under it, no scrim. (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video ; https://github.com/material-components/material-components-android/blob/master/docs/components/BottomSheet.md)
- On desktop and landscape phones (both letterboxed or pillarboxed 16:9), overlay the dock on the video in the two bottom corners and use the pillarbox bands on landscape phones as the thumb rest. Recommended: on an 852x393 landscape phone the video is 699 px wide with 76 px black bands each side; place the corner cards flush to the video edge and let the bands carry the crowd and history badges. (https://www.uxmatters.com/mt/archives/2013/02/how-do-users-really-hold-mobile-devices.php ; https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout)
- Put text on an opaque plate, not on a light scrim. Measure contrast against the brightest pixel the footage can show (white kitchen-scale bodies, LED read-outs), not against an average frame. Keep the Perspective navy glass at 85% for every text plate; a 40% scrim fails. (https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G18 ; https://m1.material.io/style/imagery.html)
- Treat the scale zone (x 38-62%, y 78-100%) and the two challenger faces as protected regions. Never place a plate, gradient, caption, badge or toast inside them. When the information is at the bottom of the picture, move the overlay to the top or the sides. (https://www.w3.org/TR/media-accessibility-reqs/ ; https://www.bbc.co.uk/accessibility/forproducts/guides/subtitles/)
- Anchor overlay plates inside a broadcast safe area: keep the bottom edge at least 1/12 of the picture height above the frame bottom, and keep plates 5% from the top and bottom edges. For 16:9 the BBC subtitle region also stays inside x 12.5%-87.5%; use that band for the two challenger cards (left card ends before x 38%, right card starts after x 62%). (https://www.w3.org/TR/media-accessibility-reqs/ ; https://www.bbc.co.uk/accessibility/forproducts/guides/subtitles/)
- Size overlay text like broadcast subtitles, not like desktop UI. Use a line height of about 7-8% of the picture height for 16:9 (that is ~50 px at 720 px picture height, ~16-18 px text on a 375-px-wide phone) and never scale below the authored size on small phones. (https://www.bbc.co.uk/accessibility/forproducts/guides/subtitles/)

## Product examples that shaped it

- **Twitch Channel Points Predictions**: A one-line notification at the top of the chat rail with a Predict button and a countdown. Predict opens a detail panel with the outcomes (BLUE left / PINK right), a points field, the points breakdown and the current return ratio. After confirm, the banner shows the viewer's selection plus the countdown and the viewer gets a temporary chat badge in the outcome colour. Lifecycle ACTIVE -> LOCKED -> RESOLVED; when resolved a chat notification states the winning outcome and points won or lost; a viewer who left sees a popup on return. (https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/channel-points-predictions ; https://stream-rise.com/blog/channel-points-predictions ; https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/predictions/)
- **Twitch Polls**: A modal at the top of the chat window. The viewer expands it with an Arrow Down icon, selects an option, clicks Vote (or types /vote N). While running, the viewer can collapse it with the arrow icon. After the end, results stay for one minute, no more votes are accepted, and the viewer can dismiss it; a 3-dot menu holds Report. (https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/how-to-use-polls ; https://stream-rise.com/blog/twitch-polls-guide)
- **Genius Sports BetVision (FanDuel NFL and 120+ soccer competitions)**: The betslip is rendered inside the live video player. Tapping a player on the footage (Touch-to-Bet) opens that player's markets next to live stats. Contextual markets such as 'Next Drive' appear at the moment they matter. Offers and alerts appear only in breaks in play. A toggle hides all graphic overlays. (https://www.geniussports.com/content-hub/betvision-in-play-betting-engagement/ ; https://www.geniussports.com/newsroom/genius-sports-launches-betvision-a-game-changing-immersive-sports-betting-experience-including-nfl-live-game-video/ ; https://www.casino.org/news/genius-sports-incorporates-betting-into-nfl-live-stream/)
- **Sportsbet (Flutter, Australia) Quick Bet**: Settings > Betting Preferences > Quick Bet slider, plus a default stake. In the slip the user taps one of the pre-defined dollar buttons or types an amount; 'Remember my Stake' keeps it. With Quick Bet on, one tap of the Quick Bet button confirms and places. Singles only; multis still use the two-step flow. (https://helpcentre.sportsbet.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/18424099184141-What-Is-Quick-Bet ; https://helpcentre.sportsbet.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/115006613027-How-do-I-use-Quick-Bet- (search snippets; pages returned 403))
- **Twitch Channel Points Predictions**: A banner at the top of chat with a 'Make Your Prediction' button and a countdown until submissions close; exactly two outcomes coloured blue and pink; state machine ACTIVE -> LOCKED -> RESOLVED / CANCELED; the resolved results card stays visible in chat only for a limited time, then disappears. (https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/predictions/ ; https://stream-rise.com/blog/channel-points-predictions)
- **Kick Predictions**: A banner appears at the top of chat; the panel shows a percentage-split bar of the current pool, the two outcomes, and a stake field. After the first commit the viewer is locked to that outcome and can only add stake to it. (https://help.kick.com/en/articles/11043577-guide-to-predictions-for-viewers ; https://win.gg/how-new-live-betting-predictions-feature-works-kick/)
- **TikTok (For You feed and Live)**: Full-bleed video. A single vertical column of action buttons on the right edge (about 120 px wide at 1080 px), creator and caption text in the bottom-left band (about 250-300 px), a thin top bar (150-200 px). The centre band is never covered. Long-press opens Clear Display, which removes every overlay; a small icon in the bottom-right restores it. (https://zeely.ai/blog/tiktok-safe-zones/ ; https://www.elitedaily.com/news/how-to-use-tiktok-clear-mode-declutter-fyp)
- **Instagram Reels**: Same edge layout as TikTok with explicit numbers: keep 270 px clear at the top, 672 px at the bottom, 65 px at each side; like, comment, share and save stack vertically on the right; profile, follow, caption and audio stack in the bottom-left. (https://www.firstpier.com/resources/instagram-ad-safe-zones)
- **Native HTML5 video players (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)**: When the control bar appears in the lower third, WebVTT cues are displaced upward instead of being covered; Chrome keeps the cue in the raised position for the rest of its duration, Safari drops it back when the controls collapse. (https://github.com/w3c/webvtt/issues/503 ; https://www.mux.com/blog/if-you-can-read-this-your-browser-captions-are-broken)
- **Twitch video-overlay extensions**: Overlay iframes render below the player's own controls, LIVE indicator and channel info; a diagram marks those zones as conflict areas; the overlay is hidden while the viewer pauses; touch targets are at least 44 pt; layout must be fluid. (https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/extensions/designing/)

## Anti-patterns avoided

- A dock, sheet or card over the bottom third of the frame. None of the five products places the vote widget over the centre or bottom of the video; Zero Sum's scales occupy x 38-62%, y 78-100%, so any bottom-centre overlay hides the result.
- Leaving the expanded panel open after the commit. Twitch and Kick both drop back to a banner that shows the selection and the countdown; a panel that stays open blocks the footage the viewer just bet on.
- Live counters that keep ticking during the locked phase. Twitch LOCKED and Kick's 'closed state' mean nothing can change; an arrivals list or pot number that animates during the 5 s freeze contradicts the product's one promise.
- Bet UI outside the player. A strip below the video breaks the single-screen rule that BetVision is built on and that the owner has already rejected. Overlay the stage instead.
- Glass over the action. Do not place any card, badge or button in the scale zone (x 38-62%, y 78-100%) or over the challengers' faces. BetVision shows offers in breaks in play and lets users hide overlays; ZSE must do the same.
- A slip that shifts or resets when live numbers change. Altenar names this as the main trust killer. Pot, player count and arrivals must not move the cards or the Place button.
- Bottom-centre bet dock or score bug. Broadcasters moved score bugs to bottom-centre so they survive square social crops (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Score_bug); in Zero Sum that lane (x 38-62%, y 78-100%) is the scales, so any bottom-centre element repeats the original complaint.
- Per-event tags or highlights drawn over the contestants. GeekWire's review of Prime Vision: 'name tags on the screen blocked the view of the actual play' and 'Our eyes got tired tracking so many circles and name tags' (https://www.geekwire.com/2024/can-amazon-change-how-we-watch-football-testing-prime-vision-on-thursday-night-football/). Do not float 'Your pick' or the reading over the challengers' bodies.
- Full-bleed colour flashes on lock or result. ESPN's Score Alert 'turns the entire bottom of the screen bright yellow' and viewers mistook it for a penalty flag (https://staturdays.com/2021/08/30/grading-the-best-and-worst-score-bugs-in-sports/). This also violates the no-strobing guardrail.
- Full-width bottom dock, scrub-bar strip, or bottom gradient over the video (YouTube/Netflix style). It covers the scales at x 38-62%, y 78-100%; the patent literature names this exact failure: 'the control panel covers up part of the video, such as the bottom portion'.
- Centre-bottom placement of any badge (Bet placed, Bets locked, pot, result). Even a small chip at bottom-centre sits on the scales. Put chips in the bottom-left or bottom-right corner.
- Mid-height left and right rails (crowd widget, history widget) over the video. Challenger 1 stands left-centre and Challenger 2 right-centre; the rails cover their bodies. Move them to the top band, the pillarbox bands on landscape phones, or the sheet's expanded state on portrait.
- A single bottom-to-top gradient scrim across the lower third: it darkens the scales that the bet depends on and still fails 4.5:1 for text over a white scale body (40% black over white = 2.85:1). Use plates in the corners and leave the centre-bottom clear.
- Glass plates lighter than about 60% under white text: navy #101A33 at 40% over a white frame gives 2.5:1. Keep text plates at the 85% stage-glass value; use the lighter glass only for non-text surfaces.
- Any plate, badge, toast or 'Bet placed' confirmation inside x 38-62%, y 78-100%, or over the challenger faces (BBC 10.1, MAUR VP-5).

## The layout (winner: Notched Quick-Bet Sheet)

A sportsbook-style bottom sheet that lives on the footage but is split around the scales. The sheet is two mirrored glass panes (navy 85% stageGlass) in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners of the 16:9 frame; the centre column between them (the notch, x 34-66%) never carries glass, so the scales (x 38-62%, y 78-100%) stay in raw footage in every phase. Each pane is one challenger: a ChallengerCard row (select) over a PaneButton row (commit). The button label carries the state: "Pick Sara" (select) becomes "Place 20 chips" (commit) and then "Bet placed · 20 chips" (receipt). 1.5 s after the commit, and in any case at lock, each pane collapses in place to a 44 px PanePill on the same baseline; the two pills are the one-line status rail, split around the notch. During reveal the sheet is hidden and the top slot of the frame carries the existing RevealChip. Results are the existing ResultsCard, centred in x and centred in the y 18-74% band so the scales stay visible under it. Crowd and history leave the frame: two 44 px chips in the glass top bar (CrowdChip shows players and pot, HistoryChip shows the last result) open anchored popovers that hold the existing CrowdPanel and HistoryPanel; the popovers hang in the outer columns (x 2-20% and 80-98%) and close by themselves at lock. The top bar is the only chrome that spans the viewport; everything else is anchored to the frame box, so the geometry holds on letterboxed and pillarboxed desktops. On portrait phones the frame is 211-221 px tall, so the same sheet renders in the letterbox band directly under the video (mode 'below') with the identical pane structure; the video itself is never covered on phones. Recommendation: build this variant. It is the only bottom-sheet reading that satisfies the owner's two demands (full-bleed footage, overlay betting UI) and the scale-zone exclusion at the same time, and it reuses ChallengerCard, PhaseTimer, CrowdPanel, HistoryPanel, LockStamp, RevealChip, ResultsCard and SessionSummary without changing their data bindings.

### Per phase

**preview**: GEOMETRY (all phases). Desktop: Stage is position fixed, inset 0. A new FrameBox (position relative, aspect 16/9, width = min(100vw, 100vh x 16/9), centred) wraps Frame (overflow hidden, the <video>) and Hud (absolute inset 0, overflow visible, pointer-events none). All percentages below are % of the FrameBox width W and height H. TopBar is viewport-anchored (position absolute, top 0, full viewport width, stageGlass, blur, ~64 px, z 3); on a 16:9 monitor it overlays the top 7-9% of the frame, on wider or taller viewports it sits on the letterbox. Horizontal inset for corner elements = max(24 px, 2% W) + env(safe-area-inset-left/right); bottom inset = max(20 px, 3% H) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom).

PREVIEW (5 s, server mode only; video held at frame 0).
- TopBar left: LogoStacked (compact), RoundChip, DemoBadge, CrowdChip (new, 44 px button: LiveDot + '128 players · 2,560 chips', tabular-nums, reserved width for 5 digits). TopBar centre: PhaseTimer dial (ring 4.5 rem) with caption 'Bets open in'. TopBar right: HistoryChip (new, 44 px button: clock glyph + last result 'S1 -13 g' or 'No results yet'), BalanceChip, SoundToggle, OverlayToggle (optional, eye glyph, 44 px), ExitButton. ErrorBanner stays in TopBar centre under the dial.
- TopSlot (Hud, frame-anchored, x 30-70%, y 10-18%, centred): ObjectiveChip (new, stageGlass badge, two lines max): line 1 = game.objectiveLine ('Cut the banana in half. Closest wins.') + TargetChip; line 2 = rakeNote + 'A placed bet is final.'
- BetSheet (new, Hud child, pointer-events auto) expanded. LeftPane: x 2-34%, y 80-97% (padding 12 px, gap 8 px). Row 1 = ChallengerCard side 1 (existing; 44 px min, portrait 40 px, Side tag, name, line, PickedIcon; theme stageGlass when idle). Row 2 = PaneButton (new, CkButtonSecondary, 44 px, full pane width) label 'Pick <name>'. If side 1 is picked: row 2 = 'Place 20 chips' CkButtonPrimary but disabled, and CloseNote (new, fontSm, tabular) reads 'Bets open in 5 s'. RightPane mirrored at x 66-98% for side 2.
- Notch x 34-66% from y 18% to 100%: no glass, no pointer target.
- Popovers (CrowdPopover, HistoryPopover) closed. CrowdChip values may tick (arrivals) with reserved width, so nothing shifts.

Phone (viewport <= @tabletS): TopBar row 1 = LogoStacked, RoundChip, DemoBadge | BalanceChip, SoundToggle, ExitButton. Row 2 = CrowdChip | PhaseTimer horizontal (dial 3.25 rem, caption at its right) | HistoryChip. Video: full width, 16:9, no radius, no border, directly under the top bar (211 px at 375 px wide). BetSheet mode 'below': full-width stageGlass band directly under the video; header line = ObjectiveChip text (fontSm); then two columns, each = ChallengerCard (portrait 40 px, name and line stacked, 56 px min) over its PaneButton (48 px); CloseNote under the columns; padding-bottom = 16 px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom). Nothing is drawn on the video except nothing: the frame's TopSlot is empty on phones.

**betting**: BETTING (17-20 s, the footage plays).
- TopBar unchanged. PhaseTimer: brand arc, caption 'Betting'; under 5 s the arc turns danger and thickens and the caption reads 'Closing' (existing, no flashing). CrowdChip: live values update each second with tabular-nums and reserved width; LiveDot pulse is disabled under reduced motion (existing).
- TopSlot: ObjectiveChip stays (same coordinates x 30-70%, y 10-18%).
- BetSheet expanded, same coordinates as preview (LeftPane x 2-34%, RightPane x 66-98%, y 80-97%). Idle pane: card stageGlass, PaneButton 'Pick <name>' (secondary fill). Picked pane: card brandFill + radio dot + aria-pressed=true; PaneButton 'Place 20 chips' brandFill, enabled; CloseNote 'Closes in 12 s · a placed bet is final' (mirrors state.secondsLeft, same clock as the dial, text style so it cannot be confused with the dial). The other pane stays idle with 'Pick <name>' enabled so the viewer can switch sides until the commit.
- Popovers: CrowdChip opens CrowdPopover anchored under the chip but clamped to the left outer column (x 2-20%, y 9-55%, width rail 15 rem, stageGlass, z 4) and holding the existing CrowdPanel (title, LiveBadge, players, pot, Ticker of 4 arrivals). HistoryChip opens HistoryPopover clamped to the right outer column (x 80-98%) with the existing HistoryPanel. One popover open at a time; Esc, outside tap or the chip closes it. On pillarboxed viewports the popover may hang in the pillarbox.
- Notch x 34-66% below y 18%: clear.
- The lock frame: when state.phase flips to 'locked', PaneButton, ChallengerCard, CrowdChip and PhaseTimer all read the same state in the same render, so the commit control and the freeze flip in the same frame.

Phone: identical structure in the 'below' band; the video is not touched. A popover on phones renders as a standard (non-modal, no scrim) stageGlass sheet fixed to the bottom of the viewport, max-height 50 vh, covering the BetSheet band only (never the video), with a 44 px close button; the viewer closes it to bet.

**betPlaced**: BET PLACED (sub-state of betting, state.myBet set; server is the judge, a rejection keeps myBet null and shows ErrorBanner in TopBar centre while the pane returns to the armed state).
- Backed pane: ChallengerCard brandFill + check icon (existing isChosen), PaneButton slot becomes PlacedRow (existing success badge, 44 px, same slot, same width): '✓ Bet placed · 20 chips'. CloseNote hides. Other pane: ChallengerCard theme disabledCtl and natively disabled (existing); its PaneButton slot becomes an inert 44 px label 'Not backed' (heading colour on stageGlass, 4.5:1). ### Interaction

Selection and commit are two taps, both on the pane of the challenger the viewer backs, so the tap target is on the side of the frame where that person stands. Tap 1: ChallengerCard or its PaneButton 'Pick Sara' calls setSide(1); the card turns brandFill with a radio dot and aria-pressed=true, the button becomes 'Place 20 chips' (brand fill), the CloseNote reads 'Closes in 12 s · a placed bet is final', and the other pane keeps 'Pick Ben' enabled so the viewer can switch until the commit. Tap 2: 'Place 20 chips' calls submitBet(mySide). The server is the only judge: on success state.myBet is set, the slot becomes PlacedRow '✓ Bet placed · 20 chips', the other card is natively disabled, StageAnnouncer says 'Bet placed', and after 1.5 s the panes collapse to pills; on rejection myBet stays null, ErrorBanner shows in the top bar and the button returns to the armed state. No disabled controls exist in betting except 'Place 20 chips' during preview (with the reason in CloseNote 'Bets open in 5 s'). There is no confirm modal, no odds-changed prompt and no cancel after commit; the stake is fixed and printed on the button. Tap on the video does nothing (the video has no controls and no tabindex). Lock: when phase becomes 'locked' every control reads it in the same render; the buttons are gone (pills), the dial is a solid lock, CrowdChip renders state.frozen; nothing animates after the 250 ms entry. Keyboard: Tab order = CrowdChip, HistoryChip, BalanceChip (not focusable), SoundToggle, OverlayToggle, ExitButton, Side 1 ChallengerCard, Side 1 PaneButton, Side 2 ChallengerCard, Side 2 PaneButton; Enter/Space activate; Esc closes a popover and returns focus to its chip; 1 and 2 are optional accelerators for setSide; two-tone focus ring (paper 2 px outline plus 2 px dark inner ring) on every control so it passes 3:1 over both a white scale body and a dark floor. Live regions: exactly two pre-rendered regions on the Stage, StageAnnouncer (role=status, polite: 'Bets open', 'Bet placed on Sara', 'Bets close in 10 seconds', '5 seconds', 'Bets locked', 'Sara: 13 g off') and a new StageAlert (role=alert: the settlement only); the dial keeps role=timer aria-live=off; LockStamp, CrowdPanel badges, ResultsCard and DisabledReason lose their own role=status / aria-live so nothing is announced twice. Popovers: CrowdChip and HistoryChip toggle aria-expanded; one popover at a time; outside tap, Esc or the chip closes it; both close at lock. OverlayToggle (optional): hides BetSheet, TopSlot and chips and leaves the dial, balance, sound and exit; a 44 px RestorePill 'Show bets' sits in the bottom-left corner as the second entry point. Motion: pane collapse/expand is a 200 ms height ease; sheet hide is a 200 ms opacity fade; both are instant under prefers-reduced-motion; the dial arc, LiveDot pulse, LockStamp popIn and result animations already honour reduced motion; nothing flashes, and no state uses colour alone (icon plus word on every pill, badge and button).

## Risks accepted

- The corner panes (x 2-34% and 66-98%, y 80-97%) are guaranteed clear of the scales by geometry, but not of the challengers' lower bodies or hands. On a frame where a challenger steps to the edge or leans over the counter, a pane covers part of them. Verify on extracted frames of banana.mp4 and water.mp4 (the rounds.md workflow) before committing to the 17% pane height; the fallback is a single-row pane (card and button side by side, ~12% H).
- The commit control is mirrored (one PaneButton per pane) instead of one button. Under time pressure in the last 5 s a viewer can tap 'Pick Ben' when they meant 'Place 20 chips' on Sara; the tap only switches the side and does not place, so no chips are lost, but it can cost the bet window. The label change plus brand fill on the armed button is the only mitigation.
- The 1.5 s auto-collapse after the commit moves the layout without a user action. Some viewers will read it as the bet disappearing. The pill receipt and the announcement mitigate this; if testing shows confusion, keep the panes expanded until lock and collapse only at lock.
- On 16:9 monitors the viewport-anchored top bar covers the top 7-9% of the frame. If a host's head reaches that band in the intro, the bar covers it. The alternative (a top bar above the frame) breaks full-bleed on exactly those monitors.
- Chrome mis-composites backdrop-filter combined with animated transforms over a <video> (the LockStamp comment records this). The panes animate height, which may trigger the same bug. Recommendation: drop backdrop-filter on BetPane and PanePill (stageGlass at 85% is opaque enough) or animate opacity only.
- ChallengerCard today uses the 'glass' theme (white 6%) because it sat inside a glass dock on a navy page. Over a white kitchen frame that fails 4.5:1. The base theme must change to stageGlass, and every badge theme used inside the panes (badgeBrand, badgeSuccess, badgeAlt, disabledCtl) must be re-measured over navy 85% on the lightest frame, not over the page background.
- Popovers are the only chrome that can cover a challenger's head (x 2-20% at 1280 px is close to where Challenger 1 stands 'left-centre'). They are user-opened and close at lock, but a viewer who opens the crowd popover and forgets it loses part of the intro footage.
- Portrait phones do not get an overlay. A 16:9 video at 375 px wide is 211 px tall; no sheet fits on it. The 'below' band is the same sheet in the letterbox, which is the previous strip the owner rejected on desktop. This must be explained to the owner as a phone-only exception with the identical component structure.
